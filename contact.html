Questions people ask before visiting

A few things worth knowing

What are your visiting hours?

9:00 AM to 9:30 PM, every day of the week, including Sundays.

How do I get in touch before visiting?

Call or WhatsApp 94123 42707, or use the directions link above — the showroom is on Bijnor Road, right next to Kaveri Dental Care.

Can I order custom or personalised furniture?

Yes — bring your measurements, a design idea or a reference photo, and the team will work out size, material and timeline with you before you order. See the Services page for how the process works.

Do you repair or reupholster old furniture?

Yes — sofa reupholstery and general furniture repair are both offered, so a worn piece can often be restored instead of replaced.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, UPI, Net Banking, Paytm, PhonePe, Amazon Pay, Cheque/DD, EMI financing, JioMoney and MobiKwik.

Is parking available nearby?

The showroom is on Bijnor Road with regular street access — call ahead if you're bringing a larger vehicle for a pickup.
