A Bijnor showroom built on repeat customers

Sharma Furniture opened on Bijnor Road in January 2021 and has grown into one of the area's go-to furniture dealers — the kind of shop people return to for a second bedroom set, then send their neighbours to.

Our story

Furniture first, sales pitch second

The showroom stocks ready furniture and takes on custom and personalised orders, alongside sofa reupholstery and general furniture repair — so a piece you already own doesn't have to be replaced when it can be fixed instead.

Staff are on hand to talk through material, sizing and delivery before you commit to anything, and the goal has always been the same: build a customer base that comes back, not just a single sale. Over the years that's meant expanding the range and taking on more repair and custom work, rather than chasing volume.
