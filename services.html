Repair & custom orders — FAQ

A few things worth knowing

Do you repair or reupholster old furniture?

Yes — sofa reupholstery and general furniture repair are both offered, so a worn piece can often be restored instead of replaced.

Can I order custom or personalised furniture?

Yes — bring your measurements, a design idea or a reference photo, and the team will work out size, material and timeline with you before you order.

How long does a repair or custom piece take?

It depends on the work and current workload. Call or WhatsApp ahead with your requirements for an accurate estimate before you commit.

What materials do you work with?

Depending on the piece: wood, engineered board, metal, and upholstery fabric or leatherette. Ask in-store about the specific material for anything you're considering.

Do I need to bring the piece in myself?

Call or WhatsApp first with a photo — staff can tell you whether it needs to come in or whether they can assess it from a description and photos alone.

Have a piece that needs work?

Send a photo on WhatsApp and get a straight answer on whether it's worth repairing.
